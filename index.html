Privacy Policy

Akshat Security Research API Test
Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Akshat Security Research API Test (“the App”) handles information when used with the Pinterest API.

Purpose of the App

The App is a personal, non-commercial development and security-research project. It is used solely by its owner for authorized testing and development, including security research performed within applicable bug bounty program rules.

The App is not offered as a service to the public and access is not shared with third parties.

Information Accessed

The App may access information made available through the Pinterest API when explicitly authorized by the account owner. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include Pinterest account information required for authentication, advertising account information, advertising campaign and reporting data, and API response metadata necessary for development and testing.

The App is not intended to collect information belonging to unrelated Pinterest users.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to test Pinterest API functionality, develop and troubleshoot API integrations, perform authorized security research, and verify the behavior of the App and Pinterest API endpoints.

Information is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or commercial purposes.

Sharing of Information

The App does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Information may be disclosed only when required by applicable law.

Data Storage and Retention

The App is designed to minimize storage of Pinterest data. Temporary testing data, API responses, and development logs may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for development, troubleshooting, or documenting authorized security research, after which they may be deleted.

Sensitive authentication credentials and access tokens are not intentionally made public.

Security

Reasonable technical precautions are used to protect information handled by the App. Access to the App and its testing environment is restricted to its owner.

Third-Party Services

The App interacts with Pinterest and is also subject to Pinterest’s applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices. Pinterest’s own handling of information is governed by Pinterest’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed toward children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
